About

Dimitris Zopounidis is a PhD Candidate at the Data Science Laboratory of the Technical University of Crete, specializing in Artificial Intelligence, Business Analytics, Banking Analytics and Aviation Intelligence. He serves as a Lead Researcher and Project Coordinator on strategic initiatives in banking, artificial intelligence and aviation analytics, bridging academic research with industry-driven innovation, data-driven strategy and decision-making.

He is also the Principal Investigator, Lead Researcher and Coordinator for the large-scale, data-driven regional economic analysis project with Alpha Bank for Crete.
